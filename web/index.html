Built for the people doing the work and the managers who need to see it.

A work inbox for everyone

View a case as any team member and see only the tasks they hold, with Start, Complete, Approve, Decline and Escalate.

Assignment and escalation

Work goes to the right level and to the person with the lightest workload. Declined work passes straight up the chain, level by level, to the manager.

Approvals and decisions

Fixes and offers can need one or more sign-offs, and a decision stage sends each case down the right branch.

One engine, any industry

Support and admissions run on the same engine, and you can describe your own workflow in the app. A new industry is new data, not new code.

Full history and undo

Every action is recorded. Undo, redo, or roll back to a checkpoint, and the stages it opened close again. Keep several cases open and switch between them.

Alerts and reports

The manager is told by SMS when work is escalated and by email when it is sent back or cancelled. Progress and escalation reports are one click away.

FAQ

Is this a simulation in the browser?

No. Every action runs in the C++ engine on the server. The page calls its REST API and draws the answer.

Can I undo a mistake?

Yes. Undo and redo any action, or set a checkpoint and roll back to it. Work that an action opened is closed again.

Can I make my own workflow?

Yes. In the app, choose “Build workflow”, list the escalation levels, people and stages, and start a case on it. It runs on the same engine as the ready-made ones.

Is my data saved?

Yes. The server keeps a log of every case, workflow and action in src/tividy-log.jsonl and replays it when it starts again, so nothing is lost on a restart. “Start fresh” under History (or --fresh) clears it.

Does “View as” check who I am?

There are no passwords: it is a demo. “View as” shows one person’s work across every case and only the buttons their role allows, and the engine refuses actions on tasks that person does not hold.

Can it run my organisation’s processes?

Yes. Choose “Build workflow”, name your organisation and workspace (for example an airport’s Passenger Services), describe the process, and start cases on it straight away. Switch workspaces at the top of the app.
